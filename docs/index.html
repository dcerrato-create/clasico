Which teams are here?

Clásico covers men's senior national teams only, in every full international match on record since 1872. Most are countries. The data also includes countries that no longer exist, like Yugoslavia and Czechoslovakia, and teams that represent regions, islands and unrecognised territories outside FIFA, like Catalonia, Shetland and Abkhazia. Clubs, women's teams and youth teams are not included.

About the flags: our match data has no flags in it. We can show a flag for today's countries and territories, and for a few former countries. Most regional and lesser-known teams have none available, so they appear with a ⚽ in place of a flag.

About the data: every result comes from a public dataset kept by volunteers, so Clásico depends on it. A match appears here once it has been added there, which can take days or weeks. We check the dataset automatically and update whenever it does.
